Why three out of four web3 games died

The anatomy of play-to-earn: three structural defects, not a market cycle.

01

The token was the only retention.

Players came for yield. Yield falls, players leave. The game itself holds no one.

02

Emission outran demand.

Rewards are printed faster than reasons to buy the token appear. The only sink is new players arriving.

03

Nobody wanted the game.

Take the payouts away and DAU goes to zero. The product was a financial scheme with a game interface.

None of the three is fixed by marketing or a new chain. Only by a different model: a game people want to play without payouts.
