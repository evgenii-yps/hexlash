01 — Essence

A combat arena
with no surrender.

Hexlash is a fighting-arena game and on-chain ecosystem ($HEX on Base). The brand is built to feel like the moment before the bell: charged, electric, inevitable. Every surface is dark and tactical, cut by a single neon strike of color. The aesthetic borrows from fight-night HUDs, competitive overlays and arcade cabinets — sharp geometry, monospaced telemetry, and one relentless creed: never give up.

05 — Voice

How we talk

NEVER
GIVE UP.

Short. Declarative. Built for the moment before a fight. We speak like a coach in the corner, not a marketer in a deck — every line is a command or a fact, never a paragraph.

We are
  • Direct — commands, not pitches. “PLAY.” “Step in.”
  • Tactical — clipped, confident, telemetry-cool.
  • Earned — respect the grind; nothing is handed out.
  • All-caps for impact, lowercase for the player.
We are not
  • Hypey or salesy — no “revolutionary,” no exclamation spam.
  • Wordy — if it needs a paragraph, cut it.
  • Cute — no emoji, no memespeak in core copy.
  • Vague — “COMING SOON,” not “stay tuned for updates.”

07 — Backdrop Motif

The Lattice

A living, mouse-reactive lattice sits behind every screen — a spotlight sweeps across it and active nodes pulse. Shards is the default (sharp, combat-coded); the cell can swap to Hexagons, Triangles or Rings for seasonal re-skins. Always low-contrast, accent-stroked, never competing with foreground type.

Icons
